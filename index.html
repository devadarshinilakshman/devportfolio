About

The Person
Behind the Lens

I'm DEVADARSHINI LAKSHMANASAMY, a Visual Communication graduate from SRM Institute of Science and Technology (Main Campus), Kattankulathur, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, Chennai. My work lives at the intersection of storytelling and visual craft — whether that's a documentary film, a brand identity, or a single photograph that stops you mid-scroll.

I'm currently looking for opportunities in teaching, freelance design, and film production collaborations. If you have a project in mind, I'd love to hear from you.
